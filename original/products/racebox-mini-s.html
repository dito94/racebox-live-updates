Introducing RaceBox Mini S

A first-of-its-kind device that seamlessly integrates storage capabilities with all the other features of the RaceBox Mini, allowing you to record sessions without requiring a constant Bluetooth connection.

If you are the occasional track-day enthusiast, a semi-professional, or just want to see how fast you can go around the track, we've got you covered. The Lap Timer functionality will allow you to measure your laps and improve your skills without ever having to stop and check your results. Read More

The Drag Meter functionality presents you with virtually unlimited possibilities to measure all of your car's performance stats. No matter if you want to see how fast it accelerates, covers a certain distance, or anything in between, you can measure it with RaceBox. Read More

Featuring built-in storage, the RaceBox Mini S provides you the ability to record lengthy sessions on its own. Effortlessly track your performance without concerns for space or mounting options for your phone. It can capture more than 2 hours of data at a 25Hz sampling rate or over 5 hours at 10Hz in its integrated storage.

Equipped with cutting-edge 25Hz GPS technology boasting precision as fine as 10cm (4 inches), this device delivers over 99.5% measurement accuracy to within a hundredth of a second, closely mirroring the results of official lap and drag timing equipment.

Heaps of exciting features to improve your driving like real-time result prediction, density altitude, bike lean angle, slope corrections, custom tracks, hill-climb mode, open-track mode, data comparison and more in the dedicated app. Read More.

All this is smoothly incorporated into a groundbreaking device that has the ability to record data without the need for constant connection to a mobile phone.

What's in the box

RaceBox Mini S comes ready to mount and connect to your phone.

  • RaceBox Mini S
  • Hardshell carry case
  • USB-C to USB-A charging cable
  • USB-C Water/Dust covers for extra protection
  • Hook'n'loop self-adhesive tape
  • Scratch-protect self-adhesive tape
  • High-grip temperature-resistant silicone pad
  • Quick-start guide

A wall adapter or car charger is NOT included in the box. Feel free to use any USB 2A charger.

The Mini S also has a 1/4"-20 UNC threaded insert for an additional mounting option.

Compatibility

You are not required to use the official RaceBox app with your RaceBox Mini S. It is also supported by some of the most popular applications for motorsports, including:

Harry's Lap Timer MX Buddy RealDash Rabbit Rally FASTLAP LITPro GPS Connector Field Navigator AutoX Data Logger

Although these apps state compatibility with RaceBox Mini S they may not support its complete feature set. Please check with the respective application vendors for additional information. If you want to integrate the RaceBox Mini S in your own app, please check out the protocol documentation.
