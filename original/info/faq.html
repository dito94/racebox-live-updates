Frequently Answered Questions

Topic: RaceBox

Can I use RaceBox on a boat/bike/go-kart/drone/something else?

You can certainly try! But please be advised this product is designed for use in a car on a track/drag strip. It does not have ingress protection, so at least keep it away from water. You will also have to figure out your own way to mount it - any GoPro compatible mounting kit should work.

Can I use the RaceBox without Cloud?

RaceBox can be used completely as a standalone unit. The things you would miss by not linking it to the application include: sessions backup, detailed laps/runs analysis, and access to the RaceBox tracks database. If you can live without these features - the answer is yes. You can record drag sessions and measure your performance and you can still create tracks and measure your laps from the device itself.

Does RaceBox support use in open-circuit mode? Such as bridge-to-gantry on Nurburgring and hill-climb courses?

Yes, we do, but with some caveats. Note that only one lap per session is allowed. As for hill-climb keep in mind, the device starts timing after the start line is crossed - if you take off right at it at a very low speed the unit may fail to start the session and mark the start line crossing correctly. In the future, we may introduce a separate hill-climb mode to address that.

Does the RaceBox support OBD/OBD2 connection?

Not yet. We have plans to add such a feature, but it may be in future versions of the hardware.

Can I charge my RaceBox with a standard USB-C cable?

You can use any USB-C cable for charging your RaceBox device. You just need to remove the magnet connector from the backside of your device.

Is RaceBox compatible with other software or applications?

RaceBox works only with the official RaceBox application, but the RaceBox Mini and Mini S are supported by plenty of other apps. Recorded data can be exported and loaded into other software too.

Topic: RaceBox Mini and Mini S

Can I use RaceBox Mini or Mini S on a boat/jet-ski, motorcycle, or different types of vehicles other than cars?

Yes. The RaceBox Mini and Mini S have specialized features designed specifically for usage on vehicles outside of the car's world. They are splash-resistant and have some specialty features like measuring drag distances from a flying start (for boats and jet-ski), measuring precise lean angles (for motorcycles), and many more. They also come with several mounting options to ease securing to various vehicles

Can RaceBox Mini or Mini S be used as a GPS tracker/logger?

No. Their purpose is totally different. They are meant to measure the performance stats of a vehicle. Functionality like this one is just not supported.

Do RaceBox Mini or Mini S support use in open-circuit mode? Such as bridge-to-gantry on Nurburgring and hill-climb courses?

Yes, they both do.

Is the RaceBox Mini or Mini S compatible with other software or applications? Like Harry’s Laptimer and Racechrono?

RaceBox Mini and Mini S are open to 3rd party integrations and various application vendors do provide compatibility. The list includes popular apps like RaceChrono, Harry's Lap Timer, MX Buddy, and so on.

Do RaceBox Mini or Mini S support OBD connectivity?

No. At the moment we do not support such functionality.

Do RaceBox Mini or Mini S require a constant connection to a mobile phone?

The RaceBox Mini is not a standalone device and requires a constant connection to a mobile phone to function properly. The RaceBox Mini S has built-in storage and can record data without constant connection.

Do I need to mount the RaceBox Mini or Mini S horizontally? Or can it be mounted vertically?

It is recommended to mount the unit horizontally or at least close to. This way it has an optimal view of the sky. Accelerometers and gyroscopes also provide more accurate data.

Are RaceBox Mini or Mini S IP rated? What temperature ranges do they work at?

The devices are IP54 rated. The accessories include an additional USB cover which does not improve the water ingress protection but prevents rust buildup in the connector. As for the working temperature - they are tested and working in the -20 to 70°C (-4 to 158°F) range.

Do RaceBox Mini and Mini S offer better accuracy than the original RaceBox?

It is a common misunderstanding that a GPS receiver with a higher update rate (more hertz) gives you better results and performance. The truth is that the most important thing for a GPS is to be accurate. All our devices offer over 99.5% measurement accuracy to a hundred of a second, compared to official lap/drag timing equipment. However, the Mini’s and Mini S higher rate does provide more data, which is crucial in the lap/drag analysis.

Topic: RaceBox Micro

Who is this product designed for?

The RaceBox Micro is the latest innovation in precision GPS technology, specifically designed for DIY enthusiasts and hobbyists. Its design enables seamless integration into a wide range of projects, whether you’re customizing an RC car, piloting a drone, racing a kart, or measuring your car's performance.

What power requirements does the device have?

The RaceBox Micro can be powered by any source between 3.5V and 16V, offering flexibility for different installations. To connect to the pre-soldered cable on the PCB, you will need a 0.1” (2.54mm) male header.

How often should I change the backup battery (CR1220)?

The CR1220 backup battery's primary function is to expedite GPS signal reception. There is no built-in indicator to notify you when the battery needs replacing. However, if you notice that the device is taking longer than usual (typically more than 15-20 seconds) to achieve an initial GPS fix, it’s likely time to replace the battery. Under normal usage, the battery should last at least one month.

If the device is supplied with a constant power source, the CR1220 battery is not necessary.

Does the RaceBox Micro require a constant connection to a mobile phone?

No, the RaceBox Micro has built-in storage and can record data without needing a constant connection to a mobile phone.

Does the RaceBox Micro have the same features as the other RaceBox models?

The RaceBox Micro offers all the features of the RaceBox Mini S.

What is included in the box?

The RaceBox Micro package includes: a carry pouch, Quick start guide, plastic case, power cable, backup battery.

Is the device waterproof?

No, the device is not waterproof. It is recommended to house the PCB in a suitable enclosure for protection.

Topic: General

Where are you shipping the product from?

RaceBox products are shipped from within the EU.

Is my data secure? Do you share it with anybody?

It is against our policy to share detailed driving data with anybody. In fact, even most of our team has only limited access to information like the number of recorded sessions, tracks being used, device accuracy and performance, and similar. We would never share your top speed or where exactly you achieved it. Detailed driving data can only be accessed when you allow us to with explicit permission and is usually related to support cases.

Is it possible to use the app with Android Auto/Apple CarPlay?

Unfortunately, currently, Apple and Android don't allow such apps to be used in CarPlay/Auto. As soon as such thing is possible we are going to add it to our feature list.

Can I use RaceBox on my favorite race track?

Even though we have thousands of tracks in our cloud database it is still possible we have missed yours. You can either drop us an email so we can quickly add it or you can create it on your own via the application or if using RaceBox - with the device itself. But first, check our tracks database to make sure we really don't have it.

Can the data be merged with GoPro to overlay your track performance data with the video?

Yes! You can overlay your performance data onto a GoPro (or any other camera) video using the official RaceBox application. For a step-by-step guide, check out our video tutorial: Video Features Tutorial.
Alternatively, you can download your session data from our cloud (via the website) in CSV or VBO format and use third-party software to overlay it onto any video of your choice.

Do you support measuring rolling acceleration tests (such as 100-200 kph or 60-130mph), etc?

Yes, we do.

Do you support measuring braking distance?

Yes, we do.

How do you measure the timing accuracy? Is it guaranteed?

Location and timing accuracy depends on many external factors, such as mounting and surrounding obstacles. We have tested on many vehicles (both ordinary and professional) and in various locations (ranging from open fields to forests, valleys, and mountains) and we are only advertising the results we get. Worst case scenario: in a mountain valley, surrounded by trees, in a car with front window heaters. Even in these conditions RaceBox still retains accuracy below 1 meter. On a sunny day on the track, you easily get <0.2meters (8 inches). During our extensive testing, we compared RaceBox to official timing gear many times and from all the results we got, the accuracy was within a hundred of a second in more than 99% of the cases.
