Can I cancel my pre-order?

Yes, any time before it ships, for a full refund of everything you paid. Contact us with your order number. After delivery you still have 14 days to change your mind.

Yes. We email production updates while you wait. If the ship date moves, we email you the new date before the old one passes: keep your place or cancel for a full refund.
