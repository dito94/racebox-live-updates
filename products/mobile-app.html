RaceBox Mobile App

Explore the full potential of RaceBox products with our purpose-built app, delivering outstanding feature set. Available for iOS 14+ and Android 10+.
Take a look at just a few of the features below.

Track Analysis

For the Lap Timer sessions, you can see all the info you need to become a faster and better driver. Check all your laps in every single session lap by lap with speed and g-force graphs, racing lines, sector times, and so on. You can even compare two laps to identify different ways to improve yourself.

Speed and G-Force Breakdown: Visualize your speed and acceleration forces at every point on the track for precise performance analysis.
Session Overview: Access a detailed summary of lap times, cornering G-forces, and max speeds for an in-depth session review.
Lap Comparison: Compare two laps side-by-side to pinpoint where time is gained or lost on the track.
Detailed Lap Metrics: Review your lap data, including max G-forces and sector times, for a comprehensive performance overview.
Lean Angle Analysis: Check the lean angles of your bike in corners to optimize handling and safety.
Racing Line Visualization: Study your driving line over the RaceBox Map or Google Maps to refine your approach.
Optimize your handling with the G-G Plot.

Drag Analysis

In the Drag Meter sessions, you can check everything within greater detail as well. You can dig in every single session and analyze the measurements of every single point. Detailed speed and g-force graphs help you analyze your car’s performance and you can easily check where you can optimize your time by comparing two runs.

Session Summary: View your best times across multiple disciplines and get an overview of all runs in a session.
Graphical Analysis: Dive into detailed graphs showing speed, G-forces, and altitude corrections for each run.
Set Point Details: Analyze weather data, density altitude (DA), and intermediate points to fine-tune your vehicle's performance.
Run Grouping: Organize results by runs or set points for easy comparison and analysis.
Overview of your drag session, where you can see your best times of different disciplines and info for all of your runs made during this session.

Sharing

Send your custom tracks and sessions to your friends. Compare and analyze your results together.

Custom Track Creation: Create and share custom tracks, making them publicly available for others to use.
Session Access Control: Manage who can access specific sessions for collaborative analysis.
Data Sharing: Invite friends or other RaceBox users to share your session data for comparison.
Data Filtering: Separate your session data from shared data for a streamlined view in the app.

Live Lap Timer

When the Lap Timer mode is entered, you’re presented with a nice, big, and easily readable UI, giving you all the needed information with just a glimpse of an eye. See your lap, sector, and predicted times, how faster or slower you’re on your current lap, and more without losing focus from your perfect racing line.

Clean Interface: View live lap and sector times in a readable format while staying focused on driving.
Predictive Mode: Get real-time feedback on whether you're gaining or losing time, tailored to your performance.
Landscape Mode: Use landscape view for a larger and more accessible display of live data.

Live Drag Meter

When the Drag Meter mode is entered, you’re once again presented with a nice, big, and easily readable UI. At your disposal is all the data you need - setpoint and predicted run times, live GPS speed, live G-Forces, slopes for every single setpoint, and even some additional things such as current DA value and outside temperature.

Data Display: Access live GPS speed, G-forces, slopes, and additional parameters like DA value and temperature in real-time.
Run Summary: Analyze a quick summary of your drag session upon completion to identify highlights.
Landscape View: View all drag session data in a large and easy-to-read format for a comprehensive overview.

Devices

Quick, easy, and stable connection to your RaceBox device. Open the app, connect and you’re ready to go! Control your RaceBox device, check its status, update its firmware, enter a session mode of your choosing, etc. All with just a tap of a button with the easy and user-friendly UI.

Device Management: Manage multiple RaceBox devices from a single account with ease.
Firmware and Sync: Update firmware, sync sessions to the cloud, and manage tracks from the device’s memory.
Session Start: Quickly start drag or track sessions, check device status, and adjust settings seamlessly.
Standalone Recording: Record data without the need for constant connection to a mobile phone.

More Features

The "More" tab lets you check the track database, create different custom tracks, edit your account information, switch from metric to imperial, check the user manual, and more. It is a one-stop place for everything you are going to need for your RaceBox device.

Track Database: Access a growing library of over 2000 tracks with multiple layouts and precise configurations.
Garage: Add multiple vehicles and assign them individually to different track and drag sessions.
Custom Tracks: Create open, closed, or hill-climb tracks on the go, using the app or while driving. Vehicle Management: Add and assign different vehicles to your track or drag sessions for tailored analysis.
Help Section: Access user manuals, feature overviews, and FAQs for any questions or guidance.

Video

Take your driving experience to the next level with our video features, seamlessly integrating recorded or live video with detailed session data for a more immersive and insightful view.

Live Video Recording: Record live video effortlessly, capturing your sessions as they happen.
Analysis: Review recorded video synchronized with the graph and your driving line to analyze your sessions.
Video Overlay: Overlay session data onto recorded or live video for a dynamic view of your track performance.
Export/Share: Easily share the overlaid video with friends on your social media or just save it to your gallery.
