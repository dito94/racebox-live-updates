RaceBox Micro Technical Specifications

The following information is based on preproduction units. Small details may change before the final release.

  • Weight: 15 grams (0.5 oz.)
  • Dimensions: 25x40x12mm (0.98x1.57x0.47 inch)
  • BLE: 5.2, certified down to 4.2. Up to 20 meters range
  • Input voltage range: 3.5 to 16V. Reverse polarity protection. Soft undervoltage lockout.
  • Current consumption: 1mA in standby (standalone recording disabled, no BLE client connected), 25mA average when active
  • GNSS receiver: up to 25Hz, supporting GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou, SBAS, QZSS
  • Accelerometer: 1kHz internal sampling rate with +/- 8g scale and 0.001g sensitivity
  • Gyroscope: 1kHz sampling rate with +/- 320dps scale and 0.02 dps sensitivity
  • Environmental compatibility: -20 to 70°C (-4 to 158°F)
  • Storage: 130 min @ 25Hz, 325 min @ 10Hz
  • Connector: standard 2-pin 0.1" female header
