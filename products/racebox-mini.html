Introducing RaceBox Mini

The most cutting-edge and precise timing gadget you can buy for your vehicle, featuring, for the first time, a 25Hz GPS!
Support for any speed or distance drag discipline you can think of - 0-100km/h, 1/4 mile, stopping distance, standing or rolling starts. Or just measure laps around your favourite circuit, hill-climb or open track events.

If you are the occasional track-day enthusiast, a semi-professional or just want to see how fast you can go around the track, we've got you covered. The Lap Timer functionality will allow you to measure your laps and improve your skills without ever having to stop and check your results. Read More

The Drag Meter functionality presents you with virtually unlimited possibilities to measure all of your car's performance stats. No matter if you want to see how fast it accelerates, covers a certain distance, or anything in between, you can measure it with RaceBox. Read More

Packing latest generation 25Hz GPS with accuracy as low as 10cm (4 inch) gives over 99.5% measurement accuracy to a hundredth of a second, compared to official lap/drag timing equipment.

Heaps of exciting features to improve your driving like real-time result prediction, density altitude, bike lean angle, slope corrections, custom tracks, hill-climb mode, open-track mode, data comparison and more - see the full App description below.

Purpose-built hardware to match the needs of any vehicle - shock and splash resistant, multitude of mounting options, long battery life, integrated accelerometer and gyroscope just to name a few.

RaceBox Mini is not a standalone device and requires a constant connection to your mobile phone to record data.

What's in the box

RaceBox Mini comes ready to mount and connect to your phone.

  • Hardshell carry case

  • USB-C Water/Dust covers for extra protection

A wall adapter or car charger is NOT included in the box. Feel free to use any USB 2A charger.

The Mini also has a 1/4"-20 UNC threaded insert for an additional mounting option.

Compatibility

You are not required to use the official RaceBox app with your RaceBox Mini. It is also supported by some of the most popular applications for motorsports, including:

Harry's Lap Timer MX Buddy RealDash Rabbit Rally FASTLAP GPS Connector Field Navigator AutoX Data Logger

Although these apps state compatibility with RaceBox Mini they may not support its complete feature set. Please check with the respective application vendors for additional information.
